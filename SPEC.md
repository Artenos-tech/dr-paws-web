# Dr. Paws – Product Features Specification

This document describes all features available to users of the Dr. Paws web application, written in plain, non-technical language.

---

## 1. Home Page

- A welcoming landing page with the tagline "Where pets bring their people."
- Quick-access buttons to jump to services.
- Cards highlighting the benefits of choosing Dr. Paws.
- A scrollable showcase of clinic locations.
- A section featuring available services.
- A testimonials section with real customer reviews.
- An introduction to the Dr. Paws team.
- A Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section.

---

## 2. Navigation

A menu bar visible on every page with links to:
- Locations
- Our Services
- Our Team
- Vet-At-Home
- A "Book a Visit" button (changes to "Book an At-Home Visit" on the Vet-At-Home page)
- Login button (when not signed in), or a profile dropdown (when signed in)

**Profile Dropdown (when signed in):**
- View Profile
- My Appointments
- My Address Book
- Medical History
- My Invoices
- Logout

---

## 3. Sign In

- Enter a 10-digit mobile number.
- Receive a one-time password (OTP) via SMS.
- Enter the OTP to log in.

---

## 4. User Profile

- View personal details: name, email, phone number, address.
- Edit any of those details.
- Upload or change a profile picture.
- View a list of all pets registered on the account.

---

## 5. Pet Management

- Add multiple pets to an account.
- For each pet: set a name, type (Dog, Cat, or Other), gender, and profile photo.
- View individual pet profile pages.
- Access pet-specific medical history and appointment records.

---

## 6. Clinic Locations

- Browse all Dr. Paws clinic locations.
- Search or filter clinics by location.
- For each clinic, view:
  - Overview (address, phone number, operating hours, parking info)
  - Photo gallery
  - Customer reviews
  - List of veterinarians at that clinic
  - Services available at that clinic
  - A button to book an appointment there
- Copy the clinic's phone number to clipboard with one click.

---

## 7. Services

- Browse all veterinary services offered across Dr. Paws clinics.
- Services are grouped into categories, including:
  - General Consultations
  - Vaccinations & Deworming
  - Diagnostics
  - Grooming
  - Emergency Care
  - Surgery
  - And more
- View a description for each service.
- Contact support for services not listed.

---

## 8. Our Team

- View all veterinarians on the Dr. Paws team.
- Each vet card shows their photo, name, qualifications, years of experience, and bio/specialisation.

---

## 9. Vet-At-Home

- Information about the at-home veterinary visit service.
- Reasons why home visits may be better for your pet.
- Services available for home visits:
  - General Consultations
  - Vaccinations & Deworming
  - Diagnostic Tests
  - Chronic Illness Management
  - Senior Pet Care
  - Travel Preparations
- Profiles of vets who do home visits.
- Customer testimonials about the service.
- Buttons to contact Dr. Paws via WhatsApp or phone.

---

## 10. Booking a Clinic Visit

A guided, step-by-step booking process:

1. Select the type of service (e.g. General Consult, Vaccination, Grooming).
2. Choose which pet the appointment is for.
3. Select a clinic location.
4. Choose a veterinarian.
5. Pick an available date and time slot.
6. Review the booking summary.
7. Complete payment to confirm the appointment.

---

## 11. Booking an At-Home Visit

A guided, step-by-step booking process:

1. Select or add a home address from your address book.
2. Choose which pet the visit is for.
3. Select the reason for the visit (e.g. General Consult, Vaccination, Diagnostics).
4. Mark whether it is an emergency.
5. Add a description of your concern.
6. Pick an available date and time slot.
7. Review the booking summary.
8. Complete payment to confirm the appointment.

---

## 12. My Appointments

- View all past, upcoming, active, and cancelled appointments.
- Filter appointments by status: All, Upcoming, Active, Past, Cancelled.
- Browse through multiple pages of appointments.
- Click any appointment to view its full details:
  - Clinic or home address
  - Veterinarian details
  - Pet details
  - Service or reason for visit
  - Booking status
  - Option to reschedule
  - Option to cancel

---

## 13. Live Appointment Tracking (At-Home Visits)

- See the real-time status of an ongoing home visit.
- Status updates include: vet on the way, vet has arrived, visit in progress, visit finished.
- Estimated arrival time is displayed.

---

## 14. My Address Book

- View all saved addresses.
- Add a new address with a label (e.g. Home, Office).
- Pin the address on a map to confirm the exact location.
- Edit existing addresses.
- Delete addresses (with a confirmation prompt).

---

## 15. Medical History

- Select a pet to view their medical records.
- See a list of all past visits, sorted from most recent to oldest.
- For each visit, view:
  - Date and type of visit
  - Doctor's notes and observations
  - Assessment and treatment plan
- View or download attached documents:
  - Lab reports (PDF)
  - Prescriptions (PDF)
  - Medical images (X-rays, ultrasounds)
- Preview documents directly in the browser without downloading.
- Browse through multiple pages of records.

---

## 16. My Invoices

- View a full history of all billing and invoices.
- Filter invoices by:
  - Date range
  - Amount range
- Sort invoices by: date (newest or oldest), invoice ID, or amount.
- Browse through multiple pages of invoices.
- Click any invoice to see the full breakdown:
  - Invoice number and date
  - Itemised list of services
  - Tax breakdown
  - Total amount
  - Payment status

---

## 17. Payment

- Pay for appointments securely via the Cashfree payment gateway.
- After payment, a confirmation page displays the appointment details and next steps.
- The app handles payment states: successful, pending, expired, and cancelled.

---

## 18. Support & Contact

- View the Dr. Paws support email address.
- View phone numbers for individual clinic locations.
- Contact Dr. Paws via WhatsApp or phone directly from the Vet-At-Home page.
- A "Get in Touch" pop-up with contact information is accessible from relevant pages.

---

## 19. Policies & Terms

- Cancellation and Refund Policy page.
- Vet-At-Home Terms & Conditions page.
- General Terms of Service page.

---

## 20. Notifications & Feedback

- On-screen pop-up notifications confirm every action (e.g. appointment booked, profile updated, address deleted).
- Notifications appear for both successful actions and errors, with clear plain-English messages.

---

## 21. Accessibility & Usability

- The app is fully usable on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
- A mobile-friendly menu replaces the desktop navigation on small screens.
- All buttons and interactive elements are designed to be easy to tap on a touchscreen.
- Loading indicators are shown while the app is fetching information.
- Confirmation prompts appear before any irreversible action (e.g. deleting an address).
